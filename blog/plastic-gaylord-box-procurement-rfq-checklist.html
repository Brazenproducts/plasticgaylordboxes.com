Plastic Gaylord Box Procurement: Building a Complete RFQ

Plasticgaylordboxes Editorial • October 2026

Sourcing bulk containers at scale is a procurement discipline, not a catalog exercise. When buyers issue a request for quotation (RFQ) for plastic gaylord boxes from Elipacko or any other supplier, the quality of the quote you receive is a direct function of the quality of the specification you send. Vague RFQs produce padded, apples-to-oranges bids; precise RFQs produce comparable pricing and realistic lead times. This checklist walks procurement and logistics teams through the data every gaylord box RFQ should contain.

Define the Dimensional Envelope First

Begin with the exterior footprint and the pallet it must match. North American operations typically standardize on a 48x40-inch footprint, but export programs and automated lines may require metric or custom dimensions. Specify exterior length, width, and height, then state the usable interior dimensions you actually need to fit your product. A box that is nominally correct on the outside but loses interior volume to thick walls can quietly undercut your cube efficiency. Document stacking height in both the warehouse rack and the trailer so the supplier can validate compression loads.

State Load and Duty Requirements Explicitly

Three numbers drive the engineering of a gaylord: static load (weight stored on a stationary stack), dynamic load (weight during handling and transport), and racking load (unsupported span in a rack). Give the supplier the heaviest product density you expect, not an average. Also specify how many times the container must be lifted, tipped, or dumped per cycle, because repeated handling fatigues plastic differently than static storage. If forklifts or automated shuttles interface with the box, note fork-entry requirements and any base-runner or pallet integration needs.

Capture Environmental and Compliance Conditions

Temperature range matters more than most buyers assume. A container that performs at ambient warehouse temperatures may embrittle in a blast freezer or soften near heat sources. State your minimum and maximum operating temperatures. For food, pharmaceutical, or agricultural contents, require documentation of FDA-compliant resin and any USDA or GMP handling conditions. Ask the supplier to confirm resin certifications in writing as part of the bid, not after the purchase order.

Specify Volume, Cadence, and Forecast

Unit price is meaningless without volume context. Provide your initial order quantity, your expected annual volume, and the release cadence (one-time, quarterly, or blanket order with scheduled pulls). Suppliers price tooling, resin purchasing, and production scheduling against forecasted volume, so a credible annual number often unlocks materially better per-unit pricing than a single spot buy.

  • Initial PO quantity and target delivery date
  • Annual forecast with seasonality notes
  • Replenishment model: spot, blanket, or consignment
  • Fleet sizing if the boxes cycle in a closed loop

Document the Full Logistics Picture

Gaylord boxes are bulky, so freight and storage economics shape the real landed cost. Ask whether the box nests or collapses for return transport, and request the nested stack height. For closed-loop pooling programs, pair the container RFQ with a request for compatible plastic pallets so the entire unit load is engineered together rather than mismatched after the fact. Specify your receiving-dock constraints, trailer type, and whether drop-trailer or live-unload applies.

Request Total Cost, Not Just Unit Price

Instruct bidders to break out tooling or setup charges, per-unit price at each volume tier, freight terms (FOB point), and estimated service life in cycles. A durable reusable container with a higher sticker price frequently wins on cost-per-trip once you amortize it across hundreds of cycles. Elipacko supplies direct from manufacturing in Thailand and Vietnam, which carry a 0% antidumping duty rate, with FDA-compliant resin and custom sizing available and US manufacturing in development.

Set Clear Evaluation Criteria

Before quotes arrive, agree internally on how you will weight price, lead time, compliance documentation, and sample quality. Require a production-representative sample for physical evaluation and a short validation window in your own line. A disciplined RFQ paired with transparent scoring turns gaylord box sourcing into a repeatable, auditable process that holds up to both finance review and operational reality.

No ISPM-15 — Export Without Fumigation

ISPM-15 requires heat treatment or fumigation for wood packaging crossing international borders. PP corrugated is plastic — entirely exempt. Every shipment with PP gaylords saves $3–$8 per pallet vs wood in treatment costs and border delay time.

How much does a PP gaylord box hold?

2,200 lbs static load. Actual fill weight depends on bulk density: grain 1,500–1,800 lbs, plastic resin 1,200–1,600 lbs, produce 400–900 lbs.

Do PP gaylords need ISPM-15?

No. PP is plastic — entirely exempt from ISPM-15 wood packaging requirements. No fumigation, no heat treatment, no phytosanitary certificate.

What wall thickness should I choose?

4mm for standard applications. 6mm for abrasive or heavy materials. 8mm for maximum load and repeated forklift impact.

Can PP gaylords be used in food applications?

Yes. Food-grade PP (FDA 21 CFR 177.1520) is available for direct food contact. Specify food-grade at quoting stage.

Specification Guide for Plastic Gaylord Boxes

Choosing the right plastic gaylord boxes requires matching wall thickness, flute profile, and resin grade to your specific application. Key specifications:

  • Wall thickness: 4mm for light duty, 6mm for standard, 8mm for heavy duty, 10mm for bulk container applications
  • Flute type: B-flute for top-load stacking strength, C-flute for balanced performance, X-flute (double wall) for maximum rigidity
  • Resin grade: Standard PP for ambient environments, high-density PP for cold chain and freezer applications down to -40°F
  • Food-grade: FDA 21 CFR-compliant virgin PP for direct food contact — required for produce, poultry, seafood, and processed food applications
  • Custom dimensions: Elipacko cuts to custom sizes with lead times varying by volume — contact directly for specifications
